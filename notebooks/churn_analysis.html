Customer Churn Analysis: Identifying High-Risk Customers and Churn Drivers

Project Objective: The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn by combining customer, subscription, and support data. The analysis aims to identify churn patterns, high-risk customer segments, factors associated with churn, and potential opportunities to improve customer retention.

Tools Used: Python, Pandas, NumPy, SQLite, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

Key Insights and Recommendations

Key Insights 1: Overall Customer Churn: The analysis found an overall churn rate of 28.6%, while the retention rate was 71.4%. 2: Churn by Subscription Plan: The Basic plan had the highest observed churn rate in this dataset, indicating that Basic-plan customers may require further investigation and targeted retention efforts. 3: Churn by Location: Karnataka had the highest observed churn rate among the analyzed states, making it an important area for further investigation. 4: Revenue at Risk: The analysis calculated approximately ₹39 of monthly revenue associated with churned customers, highlighting the potential financial impact of customer churn. 5: Support Escalations: The analysis showed a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.77 between support escalations and churn. This suggests that customers with escalated support issues may be more likely to churn. 6: Retention: The retention rate of 71.4% shows that most customers remained active, although the churned customer segment represents an important opportunity for retention improvement.

Business Recommendations 1: Investigate Basic-plan churn: Analyze whether pricing, features, service quality, or customer expectations are contributing to the higher observed churn among Basic-plan customers. 2: Focus on high-churn locations: Investigate customer experience, support complaints, service quality, and possible competitive factors in Karnataka. 3: Prioritize customers with support escalations: Create an early-warning or follow-up process for customers whose issues have been escalated. 4: Target high-risk customers: Use churn scores and churn-risk categories to identify customers who may need proactive retention efforts. 5: Analyze cancellation reasons: Review cancellation reasons such as switching to competitors or pricing concerns to identify recurring patterns.
